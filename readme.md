# Trolly's Comfy Quest Tracker (Era 16)

Welcome to **Trolly's Comfy Quest Tracker**! This tool is a companion for the **Best West Questline (Era 16)** for the *Comfy Valheim Discord Server*, specifically designed for the game **Valheim**. It helps you track your quests and manage your progress.

You can access the live version of the site here:  
[Trolly's Comfy Quest Tracker (Era 16)](https://dsthedev.github.io/comfy-quest-tracker/)

---

## Features

- **Quest Management**: Track your quest progress, and mark them as completed or incomplete.
- **Local Storage**: Your quest progress is saved locally in your browser, ensuring your data persists across sessions (though clearing your browser cache will erase it).
- **Search and Filters**: Easily search for quests by name and filter them based on biome.
- **Biome Labels**: Optionally view quests filtered by biome.
- **CSV Import**: Import custom quest data via CSV files.
- **Reset Progress**: Reset your quest progress and reload the default quests.

---

## Instructions

1. **Navigating the Tracker**:
   - You can switch between incomplete and completed quests using the tabs at the top of the page.
   - Check off quests as you complete them, and they'll automatically move to the "Completed" tab.

2. **Search and Filter**:
   - Use the search bar to quickly find specific quests.
   - Toggle biome labels on and off to filter quests by biome.

3. **CSV Import**:
   - Import quests using the "Import Quests CSV" section to load custom quest data. Simply select a `.csv` file containing your quest information, and the data will be updated.

4. **Reset Progress**:
   - Click the "Reset Progress" button to clear all quest data. **Warning**: This action cannot be undone.

## Notes

- **Persistent Data**: All your quest data is stored locally in your browser. If you clear your cache or switch browsers, your progress may be lost.
- **CSV Format**: The CSV data must follow a specific format for the tracker to process it correctly. You can download the default CSV for Era 16 [here](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/dsthedev/comfy-quest-tracker@main/quests-era16.csv).
